CHAPTER 01  ·  THE MESS

A scan finishes. Now you have 400 lines of problems.

Nmap, Nessus and the other scanners are brilliant at finding — and terrible at prioritising. Their output is a flat dump: every open port looks equally urgent, and the one that will actually get you breached hides on page nine.

192.168.1.10   22/tcp   ssh   OpenSSH 8.9p1

192.168.1.10   3306/tcp   mysql   MySQL 8.0.28

192.168.1.20   23/tcp   telnet

192.168.1.20   21/tcp   ftp   vsftpd 3.0.3

192.168.1.30   443/tcp   https   nginx 1.18.0

...which of these do you fix first?

CHAPTER 02  ·  THE RISK, RANKED

CullSec reads the mess and sorts it by danger.

Upload the scan and every finding is scored 0–100 — cleartext Telnet and exposed databases float to the top, known-bad versions are flagged, duplicates across scanners merge into one. The fix order is simply the reading order.

telnet  ·  192.168.1.20:2388  ·  Critical
mysql  ·  192.168.1.10:330672  ·  High
ftp  ·  192.168.1.20:2158  ·  High
ssh  ·  192.168.1.10:2212  ·  Low

CHAPTER 03  ·  THE FIX

Work from the top. Track every step.

Each finding explains in plain language why it matters and exactly how to fix it, then moves through Open → In progress → Fixed on your board — and exports as Markdown, CSV or JSON.

Open  →  In progress  →  Fixed

Plus: exports — all from the same scan.

CHAPTER 04  ·  THE PROOF

Re-scan, compare, and show the risk is gone.

Run the scan again after fixing and CullSec diffs the two: what is new, what is resolved, what is still open. Download the report as Markdown, CSV or JSON — the diff is your evidence, for a client, a teacher, or yourself.

NOW VIEWING  ·  FEATURES

PLATFORM

Everything a vulnerability platform should do — in one place

CullSec covers the full lifecycle: ingest, scoring, deduplication, workflow, analytics, comparison and reporting. Built by ADYREX as a showcase of modern security engineering.

◎

Contextual scoring v2 — deterministic, not AI

Every finding is scored 0–100 from service base + known-bad version + scanner severity + exposure + asset criticality + host context. Tap any score to see the full breakdown. No black box, no fake AI label — and an AI slot in Settings for when you connect your own model key.

⬡

Multi-scanner ingest — 6 scanners

Nmap, Nessus, OpenVAS/GVM, OWASP ZAP, Burp Suite and Qualys are merged into one ranked list. Duplicate host + port findings across scanners are deduplicated and merged automatically, keeping the named issue from whichever scanner found it.

▤

Executive dashboard

Portfolio-level view across every saved scan: severity mix, remediation progress, riskiest hosts, most common services and scan-over-scan trend.

✓

Remediation workflow

Each finding moves through Open → In progress → Fixed, or is marked Risk accepted / False positive. Progress is tracked per scan and across the whole account.

▦

Remediation board

A board view groups findings by status so a reviewer can see at a glance what is untouched, what is being fixed, and what is already closed.

⇄

Scan comparison

Compare any two scans socket-by-socket: what is new, what was resolved, and what is still open — the proof that fixes actually worked.

⊹

Host inventory

Findings grouped by host show the full exposure of each machine — every open port, service and version in one place.

✎

Score explanations

Every finding explains why it scored what it scored, factor by factor, with a deterministic breakdown — no AI claim, no black box.

↓

Reports & exports

Download reports as Markdown, CSV and JSON — all from the same scan.

⚑

Known-bad version detection

Fingerprints for dangerous versions — Apache 2.4.49/2.4.50 RCE, vsftpd 2.3.4 backdoor, IIS 6.0, ProFTPD backdoor — add score automatically.

◆

Saved accounts & history

Sign in once and the account is saved. Every scan, finding and status is stored under your account and waiting when you return.

⬡

Zero-install workflow

No Docker stack, no database server to babysit, no agent deployment. Scan in Kali, upload here, get a ranked action plan in seconds.

⬢

Reviewer demo

One-click fictional demo (before/after scans a reviewer can compare in two minutes) — no real network needed.

SETTINGS

Settings — only what works

Your account, appearance and demo data. CullSec sends nothing anywhere, and stores everything in one local SQLite file.

Your account

Signed in as your account. Use Edit profile in the top bar to change your display name, or Sign out there.

Your scans, findings and board status are saved under your account, and waiting when you return.

Appearance

Light or Dark is on the start page, before you sign in. Your choice is saved on this device, applies to the dashboard too, and a first visit follows your system setting. To change it, sign out, change it on the start page, and sign back in.

Security & privacy

Passwords are hashed server-side with scrypt and a per-account salt, never stored in plain text. Scan data lives in data/cullsec.db on this machine. This app makes no outbound call with your scan data.

Reviewer demo data (fictional)

Load two fictional DEMO scans — “before fixes” (7 findings) and “after fixes” (3 findings) — so a reviewer can run the full story (rank → board → compare) in two minutes without any real network. Hosts are 10.0.5.x lab fiction. Loading twice does nothing — the demo is created once per account.

Your data

Delete a single scan in Triage → Saved scans. To reset everything local, stop the server and delete data/cullsec.db, then start it again.
